Timeline

A century of
Japanese animation

More than a hundred years of artistic and narrative evolution that transformed an animation technique into one of the most influential cultural phenomena on the planet.

Full history →

1917

The first steps

Seitaro Kitayama and Junichi Kouchi create the first documented Japanese animations, inspired by Western techniques.

1963

Astro Boy — the television era

Osamu Tezuka brings anime to television with Astro Boy, defining the visual and narrative style that would last for decades.

1984 – 1997

The golden age — Miyazaki & Gainax

Studio Ghibli and Gainax redefine the limits of anime with works like Nausicaä, Akira, Neon Genesis Evangelion and Princess Mononoke.

2010s

Global expansion and streaming

Crunchyroll, Netflix and Amazon bring anime to the whole world. Titles like Attack on Titan and Demon Slayer break global records.

Today

A $25 billion industry

Anime is the third most viewed content on streaming worldwide, with active fandoms in more than 190 countries.
